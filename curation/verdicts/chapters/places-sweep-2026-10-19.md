# Places sweep: New Orleans, Puerto Vallarta, Maine, Cabo, hidden: 19 Oct 2026

Trip pools were built by `trips2.py` over GPS-found date windows (with a 1 day pad, since the real
cameras carry no GPS), then burst-grouped and laid out as 6x5 sheets labeled YYMMDD. Every sheet was read by eye.
Pick ids index into `trip_<tag>.json` on the Pro (`~/w`, copied to `_IA_review/_work/`).

(T 35, C 10, W 66, E 51, B 2, K 15). It has NOT been queued.
