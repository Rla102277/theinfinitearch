Every album below was verified by LOOKING at a contact sheet of its frames, not by
metadata. Sheets are in `curation/evidence/namecheck/`.

Status key: **FALSE** = contains none of what its name says. **THIN** = a real trip
exists but most of the album is something else. **REAL** = substantial genuine content,
still mixed.

## Camera-specific warning

**Olympus E-510 GPS is not trustworthy.** It has no GPS receiver, yet its frames carry
coordinates (Photos assigned them). In the Pennsylvania album, E-510 frames dated
2009-10-25 read 32.82/-96.72 (Dallas) on the same day Canon frames read 41.21/-77.20
(Pennsylvania). Treat any E-510 coordinate as absent, not as evidence.

## Pattern

Where a trip is real, it is usually ONE tight date block with a recognizable landmark in
it. Where an album is false, it has no block at all, only scattered single frames from
home days. Frame count does not predict truth: Nashville has 7 frames and zero are
Nashville; Galveston has 24 and 13 are real.
