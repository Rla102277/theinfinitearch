## How to read this log

Per Protocol v1.1 §22, every entry records: date, image ID/title, decision, previous → new status, reasoning, approval, and source discussion. Each entry also carries a record status:

- CONFIRMED: the photographer stated or approved it directly, and the source conversation is identified.
- WORKING: a Claude verdict or proposal the photographer did not explicitly reject; not canon until approved.
- DISPUTED: two records conflict; neither has been reconciled by the photographer.
- SUPERSEDED: replaced by a later approved decision; kept for history.
- UNKNOWN: a fact the record references but does not contain (a grade, a paper, a size).

A grade assesses the individual photograph. A classification (Anchor / Support / Developmental / Personal Archive / Exclude) assesses its role in a collection. Where the record contains only one, only one is logged.

Rules of the log: no entry is deleted. A reversal is a new entry that marks the old one SUPERSEDED. A disagreement between Claude and ChatGPT is logged as DISPUTED, not changed. The photographer alone moves an entry to CONFIRMED.
